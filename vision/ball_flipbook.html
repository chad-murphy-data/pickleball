Ball flipbook

Click the ball. It moves to the next frame. That is the whole loop.

  • Click the ball's centre. On a fast shot it is a streak — click the middle of the streak, and be consistent about it.
  • space or x — can't see it / not in frame. Never guess; a wrong position is worse than none.
  • b hold — blink to the previous frame. The ball moves, everything else doesn't, so it pops out. Best tool you have.
  • c — this frame is the moment of paddle contact.
  • z back one · → skip · save often.

Nothing uploads. Work is kept in this browser; use Download CSV at the end of every sitting.
