## Emerging picture (six independent agents, unverified)

1. **The code is sound.** Every audited statistic replicates exactly; the
   simulator, joins, filters and numerics all check out (A1, A2). The
   problems are in measurement and inference, not implementation.
2. **The venue labels are badly wrong, in both directions** (A4a, A4b,
   web-sourced): 9/23 MLP events mislabeled (all outdoor-stamped-indoor:
   Wollman Rink, Austin Pickle Ranch ×2, Arizona Athletic Grounds, USTA
   Orlando, Pictona, San Clemente, Randall's Island; NY-2026 mixed). PPA
   indoor arm only ~28% genuinely indoor — 13 events / 5,383 games (53%)
   actually outdoor (every Life Time stop except Lakeville), 18% mixed.
   And ≥8 US PPA events labeled OUTDOOR (~2,646 games) are fully indoor
   (Milwaukee Baird, Fontainebleau, Macon, Boise ×2, Baton Rouge, Raleigh,
   Fairport) + 4 Asia events likely indoor. Full tables with sources
   below — this is the review's most durable product so far.
3. **What contamination does — two readings preserved.** A4b/A3 read the
   contaminated indoor arm as making the "indoor moves too → not wind"
   dismissals partly circular (a 53%-outdoor "control" is EXPECTED to
   co-move with wind). A5 pushes back with arithmetic: under 20–40%
   contamination the indoor interaction should be only 0.2–0.4×d_true ≈
   −0.014..−0.029, which cannot generate the observed −0.080 — so the
   indoor estimate is noise or a shared non-wind artifact either way, and
   the honest statement is that the indoor arm could falsify only LARGE
   outdoor effects, never the contested −0.03..−0.05 range. (At the
   53–70% contamination A4b actually found, the gap narrows; B2's
   corrected re-run will settle it empirically.) Separately, A2 showed
   the Design-B "indoor dips too" contrast is a tour-composition artifact
   (indoor-PPA z² 1.732 ≈ outdoor-calm-PPA 1.730; the dip is MLP mix).
4. **The single strongest challenge to a published verdict** (A2, needs
   verification): the end-effects weather test used an unpaired contrast
   although all 15 windy events also supply calm games. A within-event
   PAIRED re-analysis of Design B windy 14+ gives **+0.401 z²
   [+0.016, +0.681] — nominally significant** where the published +0.224
   [−0.160, +0.585] was not. (Design C paired: +0.251 [−0.066, +0.561],
   still short.)
5. **H4 "KILLED" is overstated** (A5, quantified): the binned −2.0pp
   windy drift maps to reg-1 d ≈ −0.072; the reg-1 CI [−0.060, +0.064]

   variance channel (wind → match noise → upsets at fixed mean share) was

## Likely bottom line (subject to the pending verification + re-runs)

The original session's code was right and several of its instincts were
right, but "six nulls" compresses one strong null (H1 serve rate), one
borderline null with an untested channel (H4), and four underpowered
nulls (H2, H3-as-stated, H5, H6) into a single rhetorical unit — and its
two main null-preserving arguments (the indoor falsification arm and
"no dose-response") are weaker than published: the first because the
control arm was majority-outdoor mislabeled and statistically
indistinguishable from the outdoor arm anyway, the second because a noisy
wind proxy produces exactly the observed tail-bin-only pattern under a
real threshold effect. Nothing found so far AFFIRMS a weather effect;
the defensible summary is "tightly bounded league-mean effects at
0–18 mph; everything heterogeneous or tail is only loosely bounded."

## A2: Code audit — end_effects.py

**[MAJOR / bias: toward_null] Dummy-contrast design discards the within-event pairing; paired re-analysis of Design B windy 14+ is nominally SIGNIFICANT**

The published test is not wrong for its estimand, but it is not the most powerful design available given the complete arm overlap: a within-event pairing cancels event-level shocks (venue, court mix, field strength, log-quality) that the R:/T: split leaves in both arms. Under the paired design the Design B windy contrast crosses zero at the 95% level. Caveats keeping this from 'critical': only 15 treatment events, z² is heavy-tailed, the weighting choice moves the estimate (+0.34 vs +0.40), Design C paired does not reach significance, and the windy bin is one of several contrasts. But the session's 'CI spans zero, no end effect' verdict rests on the less efficient contrast; the honest summary is 'the best-powered version of the windy test is borderline-positive', not 'null'. Both windy groups also sit above their own sim nulls (B 1.95 vs 1.79; C 1.34 vs 1.20) — the only cells in the tables that do.

*Evidence:* All 15 OUTDOOR windy 14+ events (Design B AND C) also contribute games to the calm reference arm (15/15 overlap; moderate arm 48/49). model/end_effects.py:428-447 keys them as 'R:<event>' and 'T:<event>' — two independent clusters per physical event — so the bootstrap ignores the positive within-event correlation between arms and the point estimate mixes event composition (calm ref includes ~49 events never windy) into the wind effect. Re-analysis pairing within the 15 windy events: Design B windy−calm = +0.401 z² weighted by n_windy [+0.016, +0.681] (unweighted +0.343), event-paired bootstrap; published estimate was +0.224 [−0.160, +0.585]. Design C paired: +0.251 [−0.066, +0.561] vs published +0.190 [−0.067, +0.544].

**[MAJOR / bias: unknown] decider_splits.csv disagrees with games.csv final scores in 19.4% of rows; 6.6% have a misplaced or truncated switch boundary**

Grain errors come from pb_rally correction/rewind handling (the '~0.02%' accuracy claim in CLAUDE.md was for serve-win tallies, not these half-splits). Effects: (a) observed z² levels are inflated by tally noise the simulated null does not contain, so obs-vs-sim level comparisons are biased slightly against the null (away_from_null) — yet observed means still sit AT or BELOW the sim nulls in 10 of 12 cells, meaning the real clustering-corrected excess is if anything smaller; (b) error rates differ ~5pp across arms (highest indoor), contaminating the dummy contrasts by roughly ±0.01-0.03 z² — small vs the ±0.2 effects in play; (c) the 157 truncated-log rows put arbitrary rally windows into 'pre' — most are removed by the pre<5/post<5 filter (117 rows dropped) but rows with pre 5-6 survive.
