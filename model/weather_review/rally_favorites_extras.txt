## C. endgame contrast in a single model (to-11 games)

Ratings enter both b and d, so classical error in the v2 rating attenuates BOTH and largely cancels in the ratio d*w/b used above. Error in the WIND regressor does not cancel: it attenuates d toward zero, so the true compression could be larger than the interval by roughly 1/reliability of ERA5 grid wind as a proxy for on-court wind. If that reliability were as low as 0.6, the outdoor floor of -0.048 would correspond to a true -0.080.
