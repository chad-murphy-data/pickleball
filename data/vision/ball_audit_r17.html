One answer per frame — four kinds:
🟢 See a clean ball → just click it. Records visible (V) and auto-advances. When the ball moves cleanly you'll settle into click-click-click rhythm.
🔵 See a streak/blur that IS the ball → press S, then click the streak's center. "I know where it is… kinda" — the ball is in the pixels, just smeared.
🟠 Ball hidden behind a body or paddle → press I, then click where it must be. "I know it's right behind her" — the ball is NOT in the pixels; your trajectory sense supplies the position.
⚫ Genuinely can't place it → press N. No click, no guess — an honest N is data too.

After S or I a colored banner shows the armed mode; it resets to plain-click after each answer. Dots are HIDDEN by default so they never sit on the ball — lost it? press T to show your last second of positions, follow the trail, T again to hide. Accuracy bar: center-of-blob is fine, don't agonize; be consistent on streaks (always the center).
