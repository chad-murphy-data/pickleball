Each flight replays at half speed from just before one contact to just after the next (⏎ replays it). Say what the ball did in between:
🟡 It bounced → pause on the bounce frame and click the landing spot. That records the time and the place and moves on. B records the time without a spot if you can't see where it landed. A terminal flight (after the last contact) can take two taps — come back with P and tap again.
⚪ No bounce before the next contact → V (volley).
❓ Can't tell → U. An honest U is data too.
✕ Last flight, no bounce at all (net, caught, out of frame) → X.
Frame control: space play/pause · ←/→ ±1 frame · ,/. ±5 · [/] slower/faster · click the strip to scrub · T shows your own ball clicks (±0.5 s) when you lose the ball · ⌫ clears this flight · N/P next/previous flight.
Accuracy bar: the bounce frame ±1 and the spot within a foot or so — don't agonize. Rallies are listed clicked-first (★ = a fitter run exists to compare with, ✎ = you clicked its ball path); prefill rallies (r2–r5) have approximate contact times, so a flight may start a little early or late — the bounce tap is still exact.
Stopping: anytime — progress autosaves in this browser. At the end of every sitting hit ⬇ export and save as bounce_labels_chicago0725.csv into data/vision/ (⬆ import restores it). Score with python3 vision/make_bounce_audit.py --score data/vision/bounce_labels_chicago0725.csv
